## Give each screen a job

| Surface  | What belongs there                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Audience | The slide and its figures                |
| Speaker  | Current and upcoming slides, notes, time |
| Reader   | The complete document, at their own pace |

Open **Tools → Audience window** to rehearse. Navigation and steps follow this deck.

## Rehearse the handoff

1. Open the speaker and audience views.
2. Check text, figures, and sound on the audience display.
3. Enable **Keep display awake** if available.
4. Try Blackout, then restore the slide.
5. Return to Read when the conversation begins.
