The site now has three main doors: **Blog, Labs, and Info**. Docs and the test page sit in More. Each section answers a different question without filling the top bar with every feature of the theme.

Blog holds the story: posts, news, experiments, and the decisions behind the work. Labs holds things you can use, including the [design studio](/labs/design-studio/) and the [interference study](/labs/interference/). Info describes the project and can grow to include a team, biographies, or organization information.

The [documentation](/docs/) explains how to use Alkemist. That is content owned by this site. Someone using the theme can omit Docs or write documentation for their own project; the shared layout does not require our handbook.

The [test page](/test/) stays one long application. It is the place to compare equations, code, plots, models, and ordinary controls together. A useful lab can stand alone without turning every styling specimen into another top-level section.

Changing the structure should not erase a published link. Old notebook pages redirect to Blog, the component catalog moves under Docs, and saved design-study links point into Labs. Existing asset downloads retain their original addresses.

The [site structure guide](/docs/site-structure/) records the working routes, navigation props, and remaining file-schema proposals. Stable content IDs and portable figure metadata are the next layer; this change establishes where the content belongs.
