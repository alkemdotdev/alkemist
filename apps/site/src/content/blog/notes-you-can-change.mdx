title: Notes you can change
description: A reusable MIDI editor turns musical examples into inspectable, editable phrases.

---

A recording lets you hear a musical idea. A piano roll lets you inspect its timing, change one note, and hear the difference.

Alkemist's new Midi component combines that view with a compact note editor. It includes three local synthesized voices—piano, supersaw, and bass—and four original studies. Select a track, move a note, change its duration or velocity, and compare the result. The same component works as an editable sketch or a playback-only figure in a tutorial.

The musical source is a typed sequence of note tracks. MIDI import and export connect that sequence to other tools; the live playground also regenerates source when you edit it. The browser synthesizes sound locally, and imported local files remain on the device. A MIDI export contains notes and instrument assignments, not an audio rendering or a complete DAW project.

Try the [music and MIDI guide](/docs/music/) or the [editable component playground](/docs/visualization/#midi).
